Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Room to Work (“Room to Work,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, retains, and discloses personal information when you use this website or communicate with us. Room to Work is a recruiting organization that connects individuals with opportunities through hiring partners. We operate on a remote-first basis and serve clients and candidates in the United States.

This Privacy Policy applies to this website and to the interest form. Submission of the interest form indicates that you are open to being contacted about remote work. It does not constitute an application for employment. This website is not used to conduct background checks or to process payments.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us:

  • Interest form. Your name, email address, and mobile telephone number. Your name is required. You may provide an email address, a mobile telephone number, or both. The form also records your confirmation that you agree to be contacted about remote work.
  • Correspondence. The information you include when you write to hello@roomtowork.com.

We do not request a Social Security number, date of birth, résumé, mailing address, financial account number, or payment information through this website.

The interest form is collected through a third-party form service and transmitted to our server, where we store your name, email address, and mobile telephone number. We do not sell this information, and we do not disclose it to any other party. Upon submission, this website displays a confirmation page.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To contact you if we have an opportunity that may be appropriate to discuss.
  • To describe the duties, schedule, and compensation for that opportunity before you decide whether to apply.
  • To respond to correspondence you send to us.
  • To operate, maintain, and protect this website.

We do not use personal information to make automated hiring decisions. Hiring decisions are made by the applicable hiring partner. Submission of the interest form registers your interest only and does not constitute an application for employment.

3. How We Disclose Personal Information

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose information submitted through the interest form to other companies, including hiring partners. The third-party form service processes a submission solely to deliver it to our server. We do not authorize that service to use the information for advertising or to disclose it further.

We may disclose personal information where required by law, legal process, or a governmental request.

4. Retention

We retain interest-form submissions on our server for approximately 120 days and then delete them. You may request deletion before the end of that period, except where retention is required by law. We retain email correspondence only for so long as necessary to respond to it.

5. Security

We maintain reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and U.S. State Privacy Rights

You may decline to submit the interest form. You may also request that we correct or delete contact information you have already provided.

If you reside in California, or in another U.S. state that affords comparable privacy rights, you may have the right to:

  • Know the personal information we have collected about you and the purposes for which we use and disclose it.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to exceptions provided by law.
  • Not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is therefore no sale or sharing of personal information for advertising from which to opt out.

To submit a request, email hello@roomtowork.com with the subject line “Privacy Request.” Please include your name, the email address or mobile telephone number associated with your submission, and the action requested. We will take reasonable steps to verify that the request is made by you, or by a person authorized to act on your behalf. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf. We may require confirmation that you have authorized the agent to do so.

We will respond to a verifiable request within 45 days. Where permitted by law, we may extend that period by an additional 45 days and will notify you of the reason for the extension.

7. Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed to children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe that we have collected personal information from a child under 16, please contact us at hello@roomtowork.com. We will delete that information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be posted on this page and will state a new effective date. If a revision materially changes the manner in which we use personal information already provided to us, we will describe that change on this page before the revised use takes effect.

9. Contact Us

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy, and requests to exercise a privacy right, may be directed to hello@roomtowork.com.
