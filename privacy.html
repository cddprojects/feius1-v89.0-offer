Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy explains how Room to Work collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use this website or write to us. Room to Work is a recruiting team that connects people with opportunities through hiring partners. We are remote-first and serve U.S. clients and candidates nationwide.

This website is the interest page at this address. It does not include a job application, a background check, or a payment page.

1. Information we collect

We collect personal information in these ways:

  • Interest form. Your name, email address, and mobile phone number. Your name is required. An email address, a mobile number, or both is enough for the rest. You also confirm that you agree to be contacted about remote work.
  • Email. Whatever you include when you write to hello@roomtowork.com.

This website does not ask for a Social Security number, a date of birth, a resume, a mailing address, bank details, or payment details.

We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or advertising trackers on this website. The interest form does not send your details to a server. It shows a confirmation in your browser. If a place to receive form submissions is connected later, we will collect the name and contact details you submit and use them only as this policy describes.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Contact you if we have an opening to discuss.
  • Tell you the tasks, hours, and pay for that opening before you decide whether to apply.
  • Answer a message you send us.
  • Keep this website working and protect it from misuse.

We do not use your details to make an automated hiring decision. The hiring partner makes the hiring decision. Filling out the interest form registers your interest. It is not an application.

3. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We share personal information only in these cases:

  • Hiring partners. Only if you decide to move forward and we introduce you to a specific opportunity. We share what is needed for that introduction, such as your name and how to reach you.
  • Service providers. A provider that hosts email, or that later receives interest-form submissions, may process those details only to perform that service for us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law, a subpoena, or a government request requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of Room to Work, our hiring partners, or others.
  • A business transfer. If the recruiting team is sold or transferred, the contact details needed to continue a follow-up may transfer with it. We will update this policy if that changes who handles your information.

4. How long we keep information

We keep contact details only as long as we need them to follow up about an opening, answer you, or meet a legal duty. We delete them when that purpose is finished, or sooner if you ask us to delete them and we are not required to keep them.

Today, the interest form does not store a submission on our server.

5. How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and U.S. privacy rights

You can choose not to use the interest form. You can also email us and ask us to correct or delete the contact details you already gave us.

If you live in California, or in another U.S. state that gives similar privacy rights, you may have the right to:

  • Know the personal information we have collected about you, and how we use and share it.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Ask us to delete personal information, subject to exceptions the law allows.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is no sale or advertising share to opt out of.

To make a request, email hello@roomtowork.com with the subject “Privacy request.” Tell us your name and the email address or mobile number you used, and what you want us to do. We will take reasonable steps to verify that the request comes from you. An authorized agent may submit a request for you. We may ask you to confirm that you gave the agent permission.

We will respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we will tell you why and may take up to 45 additional days, where the law allows it.

7. Children

This website is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe we have, email hello@roomtowork.com and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use the details you already gave us, we will say so on this page before we rely on the new use.

9. How to contact us

Email hello@roomtowork.com with questions about this policy or to exercise a privacy right. A public phone number and mailing address have not been added. You can also read how to reach the team.
